## License

1. AI and machine learning: Pay-as-you-go pricing based on models, compute, and API usage.
1. Azure: Consumption-based pricing; Cosmos DB costs depend on throughput, storage, and capacity model.
1. Developer: GitHub and Azure DevOps offer free and paid tiers; .NET is open source.
1. Data and analytics: Fabric uses capacity-based licensing; Power BI Pro is per user, while Free has limited sharing.
1. Microsoft 365 Copilot: Requires an eligible Microsoft 365 license plus a separate Copilot license.
1. Power Platform: E3/E5 does not include full Power Apps or Power Automate Premium capabilities. Choose licenses based on required features.
1. Power Apps licensing: Power Apps Premium is the standalone per-user license; there is no separate “Power Apps per user” license.
1. Power Automate licensing: Power Automate Premium and Power Automate per user are separate standalone licenses, although both are licensed per user.
1. Copilot Studio: Advanced usage may require additional licensing or Copilot Credits.
1. Premium capabilities: Premium connectors and advanced automation may require additional Power Platform licenses.
